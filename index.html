Benefits of LIC Policies

Financial Security

LIC policies provide financial security to your family in case of unforeseen events, ensuring their future is protected.

Savings & Investment

Combine protection with savings. LIC policies help you build a corpus for future goals like education, marriage, or retirement.

Tax Benefits

Premiums paid are eligible for tax deduction under Section 80C, and maturity benefits are tax-free under Section 10(10D).

Loan Facility

You can avail loans against your LIC policy after it acquires surrender value, providing liquidity during emergencies.
